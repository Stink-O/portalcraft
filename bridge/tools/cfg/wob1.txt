# only move the camera". Walks, strafes, turns on the spot and flies (noclip at walking speed) over Minecraft
# terrain in the lowland west of the cliff (MC ~ -372 64 430), with per-pixel reprojection on (3 iterations), off
# (0: the old single proxy plane) and on again. pcb_wobble tracks fixed points on block faces and measures
