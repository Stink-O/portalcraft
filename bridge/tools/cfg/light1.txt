# light1: Minecraft's light on Portal 2 objects in the region (the gun, a cube). Temporary stone
# structures are built in open air above the badlands next to the cliff (only where there is air) and
# removed at the end. Marks are the eye probe's applied luminance (what the gun is lit with).

waituntil link 9000
waituntil haveexit 3600

wait 60

wait 30

wait 20

wait 30

wait 20

pcb_noclip_set 1
pcb_light_goto_mc -336.5 118 425.5 90 25

wait 200

pcb_light_mark noon
screenshot pcb_L1_noon
wait 20

wait 20

pc_mc_light 1
wait 20
pcb_log LIGHT overhang
pcb_mc_cmd fill -341 121 421 -332 121 430 stone replace air
wait 200
